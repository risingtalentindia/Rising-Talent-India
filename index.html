ABOUT US

Discover the Talent Within You

Rising Talent India is a digital platform created to discover, promote and encourage talented people from every corner of India.

Whether you can sing, dance, act, draw or have any other special talent, this platform gives you an opportunity to showcase your talent and reach a wider audience.

🌟 Discover

Find talented people from across India.

🎬 Perform

🚀 Shine

OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

👁️

Our Vision

To build a globally recognized talent platform where every talented individual gets an equal opportunity to discover, perform and shine.

Our long-term vision is to connect talents from India with audiences and opportunities across the world.

🎯

Our Mission

To discover, support and promote talented people through digital technology, competitions, events and a strong talent community.

We aim to create opportunities based on talent, dedication and performance — not background or location.
